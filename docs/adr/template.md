# NNNN — Decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Related: issue/PR/previous ADR, if any

## Context

What concrete problem or constraint requires a decision? Cite source evidence.

## Decision

What are we choosing, and what is its scope?

## Alternatives

What plausible options were considered and why were they not selected?

## Consequences

Benefits, costs, compatibility effects, migration and unresolved risks.

## Validation

Tests executed, expected acceptance checks, and any work not yet verified.
