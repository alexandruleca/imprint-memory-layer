Imprint sync relay

Two machines meet here. Their memories stay sealed.

This server pairs two machines running Imprint, one serving a workspace and one joining it, and forwards the end-to-end encrypted frames between them. There is nothing to open in a browser: sync starts from the Imprint CLI or dashboard.

What is Imprint How sync works

  • What it sees

    The room ID, the two key-exchange messages, the size and timing of encrypted frames, and the machines’ IP addresses.

  • What it never sees

    The code’s secret PIN, device names, users, memories, vectors or facts. A wrong PIN or an altered frame ends the session.

  • What it keeps

    Nothing. A room lives in memory for one session, and one still waiting for its second machine closes after an hour.

Run your own: docker run -d -p 8430:8430 ghcr.io/alexandruleca/imprint-relay, then pass --relay <your-host> to imprint sync serve.
