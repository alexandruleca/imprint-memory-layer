Both heads keep the source mouth/blink expressions, and their eyes turn as the
sources' eye bones turn them, within the sources' look-at range. They are
exported as ordinary glTF with lit materials; the dashboard lights them and
adds holographic shading and fine animated wire contours. They are adaptations
of the samples, not original model sculpts and not licensed character assets
of any anime series.
imprint-echo.png, imprint-iris.png and imprint-kai.png are previews rendered from these files.
Imprint modifications and new artwork follow the repository's Apache-2.0 license;
see Apache-2.0.txt beside this file. The original sources remain CC0-1.0.
Rebuild with scripts/build-echo-avatar.mjs; the shipped models load entirely locally.
