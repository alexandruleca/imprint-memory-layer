Import Snapshot

Live Sync

Sync one workspace with another device through a relay. Sessions are end-to-end encrypted with the PIN: the relay never sees the PIN, the devices or your data. To share a curated workspace with a colleague one way, serve it with Send only: they can pull it but cannot write to your memory. Build one by copying projects or topics into a new workspace from Settings → Workspaces.

Expose one workspace for another device to sync with. A room ID and PIN will be generated to share.

Direction

A self-hosted relay host, e.g. sync.example.com. Sessions are end-to-end encrypted either way.

Desktop App Exports

Auto-ingest Claude Desktop / ChatGPT Desktop conversation exports from your Downloads folder. Request an export in the vendor's app (Settings → Export data), drop the zip in Downloads, then scan — already-indexed zips are skipped by SHA.
