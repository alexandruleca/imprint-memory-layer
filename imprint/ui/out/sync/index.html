Sync

Share a workspace with another machine or a colleague. Sessions are end-to-end encrypted: the relay never sees the code's secret, your devices or your memories.

Live sync

One workspace per session, one way or both.

Share one workspace with another device: you get a one-time code to give it. To share part of your memory, copy projects into a new workspace in Settings → Workspaces and serve it Send only.

Direction

A self-hosted relay host, e.g. sync.example.com. Sessions are end-to-end encrypted either way.

Paired devices

Paired devices keep a workspace in step on their own: while Imprint runs on both, they reconnect through the same relay and merge what changed about once a minute. Pausing either side stops transfers. Forgetting a device keeps the memories it already shared.

No paired devices yet

To pair, turn on “Enable automatic sync with this device” on both devices during a live sync.
