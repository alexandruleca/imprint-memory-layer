Save what you find on the web into your own Imprint memory: the local, open-source memory your AI coding agents search before they work.

Open Imprint Capture from the toolbar or a shortcut you set, pick a workspace and project, choose what to save, check the preview and click **Save to Imprint**. Everything goes to Imprint running on your own computer. Nothing is sent to us or to any cloud service.

**What you can save**

- **Page text:** the readable text of the open page, including pages you are signed in to. Menus, forms, hidden text and editable fields are left out.
- **Selection:** only the passage you highlighted.
- **Link:** send the address and let Imprint fetch the page itself.
- **File in this tab:** a PDF or document open in the tab, or a same-site attachment you selected. On a Google Doc or Sheet, it exports the file with your own Google sign-in.
- **Upload a file:** choose a document or image from your computer, drop it on the popup or paste a screenshot.
- **Screenshot text:** capture the visible area or the whole page, and Imprint reads the text with on-device OCR.

The popup offers what suits the tab first. Before anything is saved you see the content and can edit the title; afterwards a card follows the capture until it is indexed, or tells you if it failed.

**Continue in Imprint**

- **Search** opens your local Imprint dashboard with your query in the chosen workspace.
- **Record meeting** opens Imprint's local recorder with the Google Meet or Microsoft Teams title filled in. Recording and transcription happen in the Imprint app on your computer.

**Requirements**

Imprint Capture is a companion to the free [Imprint app](https://imprintmcp.alexandruleca.com/) (Apache 2.0) for Windows, macOS and Linux. Install it and keep it running. The extension connects to http://127.0.0.1:8420 by itself; change the port in the add-on's settings if needed. Firefox 140 or later.

**Privacy**

- Talks only to Imprint on your computer (127.0.0.1). No analytics, no tracking, no remote code.
- Reads a page only when you open the extension there and choose an action. It never reads your history, cookies or passwords.
- Settings stay in the browser's local extension storage.
- What you save leaves your computer only if you set Imprint up to sync with your other devices (end-to-end encrypted) or to use a cloud model provider.
